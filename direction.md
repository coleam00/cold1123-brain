# Direction

Second Brain is a local, single-user notes app for a developer who learns a lot and forgets most of it: one-key capture into a daily note, and old notes brought back every day.

## It is
- One-key capture from anywhere in the app into today's daily note.
- Plain markdown files on the owner's disk, one file per note, readable without the app.
- Full-text search across every note.
- A daily list of a few old notes, resurfaced without being asked.
- Notes linked with [[double brackets]], with backlinks on each note.

## It is not
- An AI assistant. Asking questions of notes is a later release, and only with a key set.
- An importer. Bringing in existing markdown files is a later release.
- A multi-user or synced tool: no accounts, no sync, no sharing, no multi-device.
- An organizer: no folders, no tags, nothing to file or sort.
- An editor. In-app writing is append-only; editing and deleting happen in a text editor.
- A mobile app or a browser extension.

## Principles
- Capture speed beats organization. If a feature adds a step before Enter, it is out.
- The markdown files are the truth. Anything else (index, stats) can be rebuilt from them or lost without losing a note.
- Nothing leaves the machine, and nothing is deleted without the owner confirming it.
- Bring notes to the owner; don't wait for the owner to look.
